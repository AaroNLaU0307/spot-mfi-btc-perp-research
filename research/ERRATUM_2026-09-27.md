This file corrects statements in documents that are not edited in place, and it is the amendment to
both pre-registrations. It covers:

- the pre-registrations `research/PREREGISTRATION.md` and `research/PREREGISTRATION_variantA.md`;
- the decision log `docs/DECISION_LOG.md` and the pre-publication audit record `docs/AUDIT.md`;
- the generated phase reports `output/phase*.md` and `output/variantA_phase*.md`, the figures, and
  the post-hoc sections that `run_B2_pbo.py` writes (`output/phase_B2_pbo.md` and the "Post-hoc
  supplementary validation" sections at the end of `output/REPORT*.md`). These can only be regenerated
  from the data cache, which is not published (§7).

Those files stay as committed; read them together with this erratum. Living documents (`README.md`,
the hand-written sections of `output/REPORT.md` and `output/REPORT_variantA.md`,
`docs/TEST_RATIONALE.md`) and the code were corrected directly, and the generators now print the
corrected labels on the next run.

No reported number changes. Nothing was re-run. Every figure below is quoted from the committed file
named next to it; the only derived figures are day counts taken from the dates those files print.
No registered hypothesis, gate or threshold is changed.

## 1. Correction (2026-09-27): the "in-sample" statistics are full-sample

The Phase-4 grids (base M1, 42 configs; Variant A, 36 configs) are scored over the whole analysis
window, 2020-05-11 → 2025-12-31 (`src/walkforward.py` `evaluate_grid`, "Full-sample metrics";
`output/phase4_optimize.md:5`). The walk-forward out-of-sample (OOS) span is 2021-05-11 → 2025-12-31
(`output/phase4_optimize.md:28-32`, `output/variantA_phase4.md:23-27`): 1,696 of the 2,061 panel days
(`output/phase0_integrity.md:6`), about 82%. Every statistic built on the grid is therefore a
full-sample statistic, not an in-sample one:

- the base peak Sharpe 1.070 at (T=70, W=1) and plateau ratio 0.85 (`output/phase4_optimize.md:19-20`);
  the Variant A peak 1.079 and plateau ratio 0.88 (`output/variantA_phase4.md:17-18`);
- the DSR 0.959 and PSR 0.996 of the base study (`output/phase5_validation.md:7-9`), the DSR 0.961 of
  Variant A (`output/variantA_phase5.md:7`), and both BH-FDR families (0/42 and 28/36);
- the Phase-6 cost sweeps, which run on the full-sample best config.

So registered gates 3 (plateau) and 4 (DSR and BH-FDR) were scored partly on OOS data. The in-sample
Sharpes of the configs the walk-forward actually selected were 1.045–1.764 in the base study
(`output/phase4_optimize.md:28-32`) and 1.269–1.960 in Variant A (`output/variantA_phase4.md:23-27`).

Read "full-sample" for "in-sample" / "IS" in:

- `output/phase5_validation.md:5,7`, `output/variantA_phase5.md:5,7` ("In-sample selection
  significance", "Best IS config") and `output/phase6_costs.md:3`, `output/variantA_phase6.md:3`
  ("Best in-sample config");
- `docs/DECISION_LOG.md:73-74` (D2.1, "In-sample peak … Sharpe 1.07"), `:76` (D2.2, "In-sample DSR
  0.959"), `:101` (DA.3, "in-sample DSR 0.961"), `:108` (DA.4) and `:131` (AUD.1, "gate-4 in-sample
  labelling"); `docs/AUDIT.md:30`.

The earlier `output/REPORT.md` sentence "in-sample gross Sharpe 1.29 → OOS 0.29" also compared a gross
full-sample figure (`output/phase6_costs.md:19`) with a net OOS one; the like-for-like comparison is
full-sample net 1.07 → OOS net 0.29.

## 2. Disclosure (2026-09-27): the Phase-1 specification was computed on the full sample

checks on the whole panel (2020-05-11 → 2025-12-31), and `run_A1_eda.py` does the same for Variant A

Redoing the EDA on the first fold's in-sample window (2020-05-11 → 2021-04-26) needs the data cache and

## 5. Disclosure (2026-09-27): benchmarks, the base M2 model, and Variant A's reuse of the OOS windows

**Benchmark labels.** What the phase reports and figures call "BH spot" / "buy-and-hold spot" is the
perp's open-to-open price return without funding (`src/backtest.py:79`); no spot price enters it. Over
the walk-forward OOS span it has Sharpe 0.457; buy-and-hold perp net of funding has 0.313
(`output/phase4_optimize.md:36`, `output/variantA_phase4.md:31`). The base pre-registration's benchmark
is buy-and-hold perp net of funding (`research/PREREGISTRATION.md:52-54`), so the registered comparison
for the base study is 0.294 vs 0.313 (a fail, as against 0.457). Variant A registered "buy-and-hold
spot" (`research/PREREGISTRATION_variantA.md:49-50`), which is the 0.457 series. The generators now
print "B&H perp price ex-funding" and "B&H perp net of funding" and score the registered one;
`output/phase4_optimize.md:36-37`, `output/variantA_phase4.md:31`, `output/phase6_costs.md:20`,
`output/variantA_phase6.md:19` and the figures `phase4_oos_equity.png`, `phase6_cost_sensitivity.png`,
`variantA_oos_equity.png` and `variantA_cost_sensitivity.png` keep the old label. The sentence "the
strategy never beats buy-and-hold spot even gross" (`output/phase6_costs.md:24`) is true only of the
aggregate OOS gross Sharpe (0.445, `output/phase5_validation.md:19`, against 0.457); over the full sample
the gross Sharpe is 1.290 against 0.995 (`output/phase6_costs.md:19-20`). `run_06_costs.py` no longer
prints it.

**The permutation null runs on gross returns.** `src/stats.py` `permutation_null_sharpe` permutes the
position series against market returns without costs, so its observed Sharpe is the gross OOS Sharpe
(0.445 base, `output/phase5_validation.md:19`; 0.768 Variant A, `output/variantA_phase5.md:14`), not the
net OOS Sharpe the reports headline (0.294, 0.684).

**The base study's M2 model also beat buy-and-hold out of sample.** The M2 z-score robustness model
reached a walk-forward OOS Sharpe of 0.717 against 0.457 (`output/phase4_optimize.md:41-42`), higher
than Variant A's 0.684. As a sensitivity check it never went through the Phase-5 bootstrap and
permutation tests, so that figure is untested. Variant A's 0.684 was therefore not "the best-looking
result in the program" (`docs/DECISION_LOG.md:101`).

**Variant A reused the base study's OOS windows.** The base report's next steps proposed the spot–perp
divergence test after the base OOS results were known (`output/REPORT.md` §8; `docs/DECISION_LOG.md`
records Variant A after the base verdict D2.4), and Variant A was scored on the same five walk-forward
OOS windows (identical split dates in `output/variantA_phase4.md:23-27` and
`output/phase4_optimize.md:28-32`). Its OOS result is a second look at an already-seen path, including
the 2022 crash that drives it, not a fresh out-of-sample test. The calendar-year stability read
(`run_A5_validate.py`, `output/variantA_phase5.md:16-25`) was added after the Phase-4 result was seen; it
is a diagnostic, not a registered gate.

## 7. Disclosure (2026-09-27): the analysed data cannot be re-pulled, and no manifest of it exists yet

`data_cache/` is git-ignored and not published. Kraken's OHLC endpoint serves only its latest ~720
daily candles (`src/data_spot.py` module docstring, `output/phase0_integrity.md:34`), so Kraken's
coverage, and with it the cross-exchange MFI proxy from 2024-07-10 on, depends on the pull date, which
was not recorded. A re-pull today returns different data, so no published number can be re-derived
exactly.

From this date every pull records `data_cache/MANIFEST.json` (file, sha256, rows, first/last timestamp,
pull time in UTC; `src/snapshot.py`), `run_00_data.py` prints it in the Phase-0 report, and the loaders
refuse a cached raw file that does not match it. The manifest of the cache the published results were
computed from can only be produced on the machine that holds that cache (`python -m src.snapshot`,
with `--pulled-at` if the pull time can be established). That step is pending; until it is done, every
number in `output/` is as reported, not reproducible from a pinned snapshot.
