This file corrects statements in documents that are not edited in place, and it is the amendment to
both pre-registrations. It covers:

- the pre-registrations `research/PREREGISTRATION.md` and `research/PREREGISTRATION_variantA.md`;
- the decision log `docs/DECISION_LOG.md` and the pre-publication audit record `docs/AUDIT.md`;
- the generated phase reports `output/phase*.md` and `output/variantA_phase*.md`, the figures, and
  the post-hoc sections that `run_B2_pbo.py` writes (`output/phase_B2_pbo.md` and the "Post-hoc
  supplementary validation" sections at the end of `output/REPORT*.md`). These can only be regenerated

Those files stay as committed; read them together with this erratum. Living documents (`README.md`,
the hand-written sections of `output/REPORT.md` and `output/REPORT_variantA.md`,

named next to it; the only derived figures are day counts taken from the dates those files print.
No registered hypothesis, gate or threshold is changed.

## 1. Correction (2026-09-27): the "in-sample" statistics are full-sample

The Phase-4 grids (base M1, 42 configs; Variant A, 36 configs) are scored over the whole analysis
window, 2020-05-11 → 2025-12-31 (`src/walkforward.py` `evaluate_grid`, "Full-sample metrics";
`output/phase4_optimize.md:5`). The walk-forward out-of-sample (OOS) span is 2021-05-11 → 2025-12-31
(`output/phase4_optimize.md:28-32`, `output/variantA_phase4.md:23-27`): 1,696 of the 2,061 panel days
(`output/phase0_integrity.md:6`), about 82%. Every statistic built on the grid is therefore a
full-sample statistic, not an in-sample one:

- the base peak Sharpe 1.070 at (T=70, W=1) and plateau ratio 0.85 (`output/phase4_optimize.md:19-20`);
  the Variant A peak 1.079 and plateau ratio 0.88 (`output/variantA_phase4.md:17-18`);
- the DSR 0.959 and PSR 0.996 of the base study (`output/phase5_validation.md:7-9`), the DSR 0.961 of
  Variant A (`output/variantA_phase5.md:7`), and both BH-FDR families (0/42 and 28/36);
- the Phase-6 cost sweeps, which run on the full-sample best config.

So registered gates 3 (plateau) and 4 (DSR and BH-FDR) were scored partly on OOS data. The in-sample
Sharpes of the configs the walk-forward actually selected were 1.045–1.764 in the base study
(`output/phase4_optimize.md:28-32`) and 1.269–1.960 in Variant A (`output/variantA_phase4.md:23-27`).

Read "full-sample" for "in-sample" / "IS" in:

- `output/phase5_validation.md:5,7`, `output/variantA_phase5.md:5,7` ("In-sample selection
  significance", "Best IS config") and `output/phase6_costs.md:3`, `output/variantA_phase6.md:3`
  ("Best in-sample config");
- `docs/DECISION_LOG.md:73-74` (D2.1, "In-sample peak … Sharpe 1.07"), `:76` (D2.2, "In-sample DSR
  0.959"), `:101` (DA.3, "in-sample DSR 0.961"), `:108` (DA.4) and `:131` (AUD.1, "gate-4 in-sample
  labelling"); `docs/AUDIT.md:30`.

The earlier `output/REPORT.md` sentence "in-sample gross Sharpe 1.29 → OOS 0.29" also compared a gross
full-sample figure (`output/phase6_costs.md:19`) with a net OOS one; the like-for-like comparison is
full-sample net 1.07 → OOS net 0.29.

## 2. Disclosure (2026-09-27): the Phase-1 specification was computed on the full sample

`run_01_eda.py:35-44` computes the IC table, the non-overlapping IC, the deciles and the stationarity
checks on the whole panel (2020-05-11 → 2025-12-31), and `run_A1_eda.py` does the same for Variant A
from line 38. Those readings fixed the direction (momentum, not mean reversion), the long-only choice
and the grid ranges registered in `research/PREREGISTRATION.md:13-46` and
`research/PREREGISTRATION_variantA.md:27-45`. The Phase-1 sample contains the entire walk-forward OOS
span, so the specification was informed by OOS-period data, and the walk-forward OOS is not an
untouched test. The pre-registrations' statement that the direction was chosen "from the IC sign, not
from PnL" remains true.

This can only make the hypotheses look better out of sample. Both studies still failed registered
out-of-sample gates, so `FALSIFIED` (base) and `INCONCLUSIVE`, leaning `FALSIFIED` (Variant A) stand.
Redoing the EDA on the first fold's in-sample window (2020-05-11 → 2021-04-26) needs the data cache and
has not been done.

## 3. Correction (2026-09-27): 3 of the 7 registered gates pass in the base study, not 2 of 7

The base pre-registration registers seven gates (`research/PREREGISTRATION.md:57-66`). The previous
gate table in `output/REPORT.md` split registered gate 5 (bootstrap CI **and** permutation null) into
two rows and dropped registered gate 6 ("Aggregated walk-forward OOS equity is positive"). Gate 6
passes on the published statistics: aggregated OOS annualised return +4.4%, net Sharpe 0.294
(`output/phase4_optimize.md:35`). On the registered list, gates 3, 6 and 7 pass and gates 1, 2, 4 and 5
fail. The verdict is unchanged: one failed gate is enough for `FALSIFIED`.

Registered gate 2 compares with the registered benchmark, buy-and-hold BTCUSDT perp net of funding
(`research/PREREGISTRATION.md:52-54`), which is 0.313 over the OOS span (`output/phase4_optimize.md:36`).
The previous table used "buy-and-hold spot" 0.457, which the engine computes as the perp's
open-to-open price return without funding (`src/backtest.py:79`). The OOS Sharpe 0.294 is below both,
so gate 2 fails either way.

The gate tables in `output/REPORT.md` and `output/REPORT_variantA.md` are now generated by
`src/gates.py` from the statistics published in the phase-4/5/6 reports, with the gate lists taken from
the pre-registrations and the thresholds read from `config.py` (where they were defined before but
never read). `run_06_costs.py` and `run_A6_costs.py` regenerate the tables; `python -m src.gates` does
the same from the committed reports. Variant A's result is unchanged: 5 of 7 pass, gates 5 and 6 fail.

Read 3 pass / 4 fail in `docs/DECISION_LOG.md:123-125` (AUD.1(c)) and `docs/AUDIT.md:22`, which
describe the previous table as "one row per pre-registered gate … 2 pass / 5 fail". `docs/DECISION_LOG.md:82-83`
(D2.4) names five failures; they are four registered gates, because the bootstrap CI and the
permutation null form one gate.

## 4. Correction (2026-09-27): 127 configurations were tested, not 78

`output/phase_B2_pbo.md:67` (written by `run_B2_pbo.py`) and `docs/AUDIT.md:110` give 42 (base) + 36
(Variant A) = 78 configurations. Phase 4 of the base study also evaluated and walk-forwarded the M2
z-score robustness grid (`run_04_optimize.py`, "M2 robustness"; `config.GRID_Z_THRESHOLDS` ×
`config.GRID_Z_WINDOWS`, 7 × 7 = 49; results at `output/phase4_optimize.md:39-42`). The program total
is 42 + 49 + 36 = 127. The 49 M2 configs are outside both BH-FDR families and both DSR trial counts
(N = 42, N = 36). A wider family can only raise the significance bar, so neither verdict changes.
`run_B2_pbo.py` now counts the configurations from the grids themselves.
